# Tableau-Sales-Customer-Analytics-Dashboard-
An interactive Tableau project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, and customer behavior.


Key Features
----------------

Sales Analysis: Track total sales, profit, quantity, and weekly trends.

Category Performance: Compare sales and profit across product categories and sub-categories.

Customer Insights: Analyze customer count, sales per customer, and order frequency.

Top 10 Customers: Identify the most profitable customers based on sales, profit, and orders.

Year-over-Year Analysis: Compare business performance across 2020–2023.

Interactive Filters: Explore data by year, category, sub-category, region, state, and city.



Tools & Technologies
----------------------

-- Tableau

-- Data Visualization

-- Business Intelligence

-- Sales and Customer Analytics

Objective
-----------

To transform sales and customer data into actionable insights that support data-driven decision-making, improve profitability, and 

identify opportunities for business growth.
